# Colonial Singapore

::::page{template="idea-lead" id="source-18"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

Singapore may have gained full independence in 1965, but its architecture and street names still nod to its colonial history. Take a trip along the streets of the downtown area – like the British, Singaporeans drive on the left – and you’ll pass the churches and administrative buildings of colonial rule. Increasingly, these are morphing into arts centres, hotels and museums, ensuring that their impressive colonnaded facades are preserved for future generations.
