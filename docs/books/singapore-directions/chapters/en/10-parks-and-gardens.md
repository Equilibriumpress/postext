Heavily urbanized and industrialized it may be, but Singapore still retains some verdant pockets. Indeed, even as you make your way into town from the airport, you’ll be struck by the lushness of its landscape. But to experience Singapore’s most beautiful scenery, you’ll want to head for one of its dedicated spaces, where centuries-old trees tower over tropical blooms, and palm trees shade pristine beaches.
