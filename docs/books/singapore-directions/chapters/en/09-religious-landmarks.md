# Religious landmarks

::::page{template="idea-lead" id="source-22"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

Few other places of its size in the world have as much religious diversity as Singapore. The faiths of the Chinese, Malays, Europeans and Indians that make up the Singaporean population have informed the community since its fi rst days, leaving the island liberally sprinkled with temples, churches, mosques, gurdwaras and synagogues. All welcome visitors, though be sure to pay heed to any signs detailing worship times and dress codes, at the entrance.
