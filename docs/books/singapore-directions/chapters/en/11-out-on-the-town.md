The last decade has witnessed a huge evolution of the Singapore bar and club scene, meaning that a night’s carousing no longer revolves around staid or tacky hotel venues. Whether you prefer to relax at a venerable colonial- style watering hole, or to don your dancing shoes for a night in a club, you’re sure to fi nd a joint to suit you.
