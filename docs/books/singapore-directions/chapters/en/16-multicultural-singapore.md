All corners of Asia meet in multicultural Singapore: in the space of a couple of hours, you could tuck into a south Indian curry served on a banana leaf, browse for silks and linens in an Arab Quarter textile store, and pop into a Buddhist temple to watch worshippers offer up incense to their ancestors. Local tour guide fi rms will whisk you from one ethnic enclave to another; but in truth there’s no better way to soak up the atmosphere than to stroll around independently.
