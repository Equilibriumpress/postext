# Shops & markets

::::page{template="idea-lead" id="source-32"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

The Singaporean love affair with shopping has spawned a giddying range of outlets and products. In the gleaming shopping malls of Orchard Road, you’ll fi nd boutiques and department stores selling everything from pricey antiques to the sharpest designer brands. At the other end of the price scale, Singapore’s markets offer an appealing taster of city life. If you’re happy to haggle, head for the ethnic enclaves, where you can take your pick of handcrafted textiles, jewellery, artefacts and all sorts of knick-knacks.
