Long something of a cultural desert, Singapore has in recent times witnessed a burgeoning arts scene that refl ects the government’s intention to become the region’s cultural capital. Since the opening of the Theatres on the Bay complex early in the new millennium, the artistic scene hasn’t looked back, and these days you can catch music, drama and fi lm at a broad range of venues across the city. There’s an expanding roster of high-profi le arts festivals, too.
