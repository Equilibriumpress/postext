# Viewpoints

::::page{template="idea-lead" id="source-48"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

With its soaring skyscrapers, Singapore has plenty of vantage points from which you can enjoy vistas that will stop you dead in your tracks. From the upper fl oors of the Swissôtel, for example, you can clearly see as far as southern Malaysia and Indonesia’s Riau Archipelago, while from Mount Faber, out west, or the cable car to Sentosa, there are stunning views back into the downtown district.
