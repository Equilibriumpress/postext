# Singapore dining

::::page{template="idea-lead" id="source-20"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

Singaporeans live to eat, and thousands of restaurants and food stalls allow them to indulge their obsession. Since the city was built upon migration, there is no truly indigenous Singaporean cuisine, but residents have taken dishes such as Hainanese chicken rice, satay, chilli crab and fi sh- head curry to their hearts, and all stake a claim to national dish status. These days, Singapore can boast design-led gourmet restaurants to match anything London, Paris or New York has to offer; but for the quintessential dining experience, you’ll want to head for a hot and smoky hawker centre.
