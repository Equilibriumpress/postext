:::frame{slot="story"}

Dinas Head projects into the sea between Fishguard and Newport. Two small bays sit at the ends of a low valley, with higher ground rising to the north. The short inland link and the exposed coastal circuit offer different experiences of the same landscape.

Begin by looking at the shape of the headland rather than counting the miles.
