05 / ROUTE NOTES

:::

# Four moments on the path {style="page-heading"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

A compact circuit offers several distinct experiences. Use these notes alongside the map.

:::

:::frame{slot="card"}

**01 · Cwm-yr-Eglwys**

Begin at the eastern bay. Notice the church remains and the low ground behind the shore.

:::

:::frame{slot="card"}

**02 · Pwllgwaelod**

Cross through the valley to the western cove. The outer path begins to climb from here.

:::

:::frame{slot="card"}

**03 · The headland**

Follow the established coast path over higher ground. Stop only where the path is wide and safe.

:::

:::frame{slot="card"}

**04 · The eastern slope**

Descend along the established trail towards the eastern bay. Allow time for steps and uneven ground.

:::

:::

:::frame{slot="right"}

:::
