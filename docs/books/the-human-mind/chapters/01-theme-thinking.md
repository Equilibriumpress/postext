### What the books investigate

Judgment is not a single error to be repaired. A fast impression, an unnoticed detail and unwanted disagreement between reviewers are different problems. A correction that helps with one may do nothing for the others.

## Five books. Five different lenses.
:::
:::frame{slot="lead"}
Kahneman explains shortcuts that make a plausible answer feel complete. Chabris and Simons show what attention can leave outside awareness. Noise asks whether different decision-makers reach inconsistent answers from identical evidence. Galef and McRaney then ask how a belief is defended, questioned or revised. Together, these books offer different safeguards rather than one prescription for rationality.
