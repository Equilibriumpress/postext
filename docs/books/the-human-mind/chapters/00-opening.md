HOW TO READ THIS BOOK

:::frame{slot="title"}

:::
:::frame{slot="step1"}
**See the argument.**

Original diagrams are paired with documented cases or clearly labelled illustrative scenes.
:::
:::frame{slot="step2num"}
02
:::
:::frame{slot="step2"}
**Read eight ideas.**

Eight insights are connected to one author, with evidence limits and book-specific context.
:::
:::frame{slot="step3num"}
03
:::
:::frame{slot="step3"}
**Try two things.**

Two executable prompts relate to the argument. They are for reflection, not treatment.

:::frame{slot="foot"}
**The full edition**

Six double-page introductions compare five books per theme, from judgment and learning to emotion, motivation, relationships and the brain. Real cases are distinguished from invented teaching examples. Read the source register for publication details and consult the complete books before treating an interpretation as evidence.
