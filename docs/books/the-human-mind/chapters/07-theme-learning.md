# Memory & Learning

## Why does familiar information disappear when you need it?

AN EVERYDAY QUESTION · ILLUSTRATIVE SCENE

A student rereads a page three times and underlines the important sentences. During a conversation the next day, the explanation will not come. A colleague who tested herself without notes remembers more, though practice felt less comfortable.

### What the books investigate

The feeling of fluency is not the same as durable learning. Remembering relies on attention and retrieval, while understanding also depends on what a learner already knows.

## Five books. Five different lenses.

Make It Stick examines retrieval and spaced practice. Remember asks how memories are formed and lost. The Organized Mind considers where attention goes when demands compete. The Extended Mind broadens cognition to useful external support. Willingham brings background knowledge and instruction into the picture. These are complementary, not interchangeable theories of learning.
