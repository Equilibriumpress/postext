COLOPHON / INDEPENDENT EDITION

# A guide, not the final word.

Read authors critically. Check the evidence. Keep asking questions.

## Method

Thirty books, each with eight independently written insights, two practical ideas and an original infographic. No copyrighted diagrams or substantial original passages are reproduced.

## Scientific distinctions

Conceptual models simplify complex research. Emotion construction, cognitive systems, stress responses and neuroscience include active debates. The illustrations contain no invented experimental measurements.

## Source limits

Book identities and publisher or author overviews have been checked. An exhaustive page-by-page audit against every complete original book, and independent scientific peer review, remain outstanding. Read original works for the full argument.
