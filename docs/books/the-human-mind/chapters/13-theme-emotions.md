### What the books investigate

An emotion is an experience, not a complete instruction. Its causes, interpretation and consequences are different questions, and the books in this group disagree on parts of the explanation.

## Five books. Five different lenses.
:::
:::frame{slot="lead"}
Barrett investigates how emotions are constructed. David focuses on how to respond without being ruled by a feeling. Gilbert explores the unreliability of forecasts about future happiness. Haidt asks a broader question about a good life. McGonigal examines expectations surrounding stress. Their approaches should not be merged into one promise of emotional control.
