# Along the way {style="display-heading"}

:::frame{slot="intro"}

Five stages describe the circuit from the eastern bay, through the valley and around the headland.

:::

:::frame{slot="n1"}

01

:::

:::frame{slot="s1"}

**Cwm-yr-Eglwys**

From the beach, take the inland link towards Pwllgwaelod through the sheltered ground behind the peninsula.

:::

:::frame{slot="n2"}

02

:::

:::frame{slot="s2"}

**Pwllgwaelod beach**

From the western cove, follow the coastal lane uphill. Where the lane curves right, use the gate on the left to regain the established headland path.

:::

:::frame{slot="n3"}

03

:::

:::frame{slot="s3"}

**Above Fishguard Bay**

Stay on the stepped upper trail. Ignore narrow informal tracks dropping towards the cliff edge. Continue towards the open high ground.

:::

:::frame{slot="n4"}

04

:::

:::frame{slot="s4"}

**The high ground**

Keep the sea outside the circuit as the path turns towards Newport Bay. Follow the established trail, rather than informal tracks across the grass.

:::

:::frame{slot="n5"}

05

:::

:::frame{slot="s5"}

**Eastern descent**

Descend the established steps and cross the footbridge towards Cwm-yr-Eglwys. The church ruins mark the return to the starting bay.

:::

:::frame{slot="note"}

Short does not mean sheltered. Expect steps, uneven ground and mud after rain. Turn back if conditions are unsuitable.

:::
