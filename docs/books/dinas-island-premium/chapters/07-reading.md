# A slower walk {style="flow-opener" kicker="08 / FIELD ESSAY"}

Dinas Island is a good place to practise reading the coast. The valley, two bays, high path and church remains bring several different landscapes into one short outing. Start with the map, then pay attention to the changes in the ground as you walk.

## Before leaving

Read current route advice and check the weather. Carry suitable footwear, water and a charged phone. Decide whether the exposed circuit suits your group. The sheltered crossing between the bays is a useful alternative.

## Along the way

Notice how the wind feels in the valley and on the higher ground. Look at the water and the vegetation beside the path, without approaching cliff edges or disturbing wildlife. At the church, consider how the site relates to the sea. The remains date from the destruction of the building in the storm of 1859.

## On returning

Compare the photograph and the terrain diagram with what you saw. Where did the route feel exposed? Where did it offer shelter? The distance has not changed, but your understanding of the landscape has.
