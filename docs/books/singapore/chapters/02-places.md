# Marina Bay
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="bay"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Merlion Park**

A compact viewpoint across the bay. Approach on foot from the Civic District, passing the river mouth. Cross Helix Bridge for a changing view of the waterfront.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Marina Bay Sands**

The hotel, mall and museum sit on the south side. The exterior architecture is worth seeing even without a ticket.

# Civic District
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="museum"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Padang & National Gallery**

Begin beside the open field where public buildings frame the historic centre. The former Supreme Court and City Hall house art from Singapore and the region.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Singapore River**

Follow the riverside towards Boat Quay and look back towards the civic buildings.

# Chinatown
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="temple"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**South Bridge Road**

Religious buildings and commercial streets share a compact district. Pause at the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple. Look at shutters and covered walkways.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Maxwell & Chinatown Complex**

Both are useful places to explore hawker food. Allow time to browse before choosing a stall. Avoid blocking narrow entrances and paths.

# Kampong Gelam
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="mosque"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Sultan Mosque**

The gold dome anchors the view down Bussorah Street. Check visiting arrangements. Textile shops on Arab Street reflect the area's trading history.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Haji Lane**

A narrow street of independent shops and cafés. Visit at a quiet hour to appreciate its scale. Continue towards the wider Bugis district.

# Little India

:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="shophouses"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Serangoon Road**

The main street links shops, restaurants and religious landmarks. Explore side streets too. Tekka Centre is useful for food and market activity.

:::frame{slot="title"}
# Botanic Gardens
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-places"}
:::
:::frame{slot="art"}
::resource{id="green"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**A tropical landscape**

The UNESCO-listed gardens contain lawns, lakes and collections. Take a shaded route and allow time for pauses. The National Orchid Garden is a separate attraction.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Planning**

Choose an entrance according to the gardens you want to visit. Bring water and check opening hours.
