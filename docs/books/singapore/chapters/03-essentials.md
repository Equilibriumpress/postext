# Getting around
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-essentials"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**MRT, buses & walking**

Rail is useful between districts. Choose an exit using station signs. Buses reach residential and coastal areas. Use marked crossings and covered walkways.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Payment & accessibility**

Check accepted cards and fare rules before travelling. Confirm individual station lifts and step-free access.

[Land Transport Authority](https://www.lta.gov.sg/)

# Weather & timing
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-essentials"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Tropical weather**

Warm, humid conditions make shade and water important. Schedule outdoor walks early and use museums or indoor attractions during the hotter part of the day.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**During storms**

Follow official weather advice and seek appropriate shelter during lightning. Confirm opening days and timed-entry requirements.

[Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov.sg/)

# Eating & drinking
:::
:::frame{slot="tab"}
::resource{id="tab-essentials"}
:::
:::frame{slot="left"}
**Hawker centres**

Read menus, queue at the stall and return trays according to local signs. Payment arrangements differ by vendor. Ask about allergens and dietary requirements.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Water and breaks**

Keep water with you for outdoor visits. Plan regular rests in shade or air-conditioned spaces. Check last orders and transport before late-night trips.
