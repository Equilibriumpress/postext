**Day one**

Walk from the civic buildings to the Merlion and across the bay. The waterfront gives a useful introduction to the city.
:::
:::frame{slot="right"}
**Days two and three**

Combine Chinatown and Kampong Gelam, then spend a morning among the Botanic Gardens' trees. Keep an indoor alternative for wet weather.
